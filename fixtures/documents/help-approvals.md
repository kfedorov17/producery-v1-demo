## How does the approval queue work?

Every outbound message and every record change the assistant proposes becomes an approval request. Nothing is sent or written until a licensed producer decides. Each request shows the draft, who it goes to, the owner brief, and the compliance flags.

## What do the three decisions mean?

- Approve — the draft goes out exactly as written.
- Edit and approve — change the text first; your version is what is sent, and your edit is remembered as a procedural preference that shapes future drafts.
- Reject — nothing is sent. Add a reason if you want the assistant to stop proposing this kind of message; the reason is remembered.

## Can anything ever send without approval?

No. Everything with judgment in it — quotes, renewal terms, advice — always waits for a human decision.
