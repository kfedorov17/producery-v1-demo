HARBOR SPECIALTY UNDERWRITERS - MANAGING GENERAL AGENCY PRODUCER AGREEMENT

1. APPOINTMENT
Harbor Specialty Underwriters ('Harbor') appoints the Producer to solicit and submit applications for the commercial lines programs Harbor administers.

2. BOOK OF BUSINESS
Expirations and renewal rights on business submitted by the Producer remain the property of the Producer. Harbor acquires no ownership interest in the Producer's accounts.

3. COMPENSATION
Harbor shall pay the Producer a commission of twelve percent (12%) of written premium on new and renewal business, payable monthly net of return premiums.

4. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice. Termination does not affect commissions on policies in force at termination.

5. NON-COMPETITION
No non-competition or non-solicitation covenant applies to the Producer under this Agreement.

6. ERRORS & OMISSIONS
The Producer shall maintain errors and omissions coverage of at least $1,000,000 per claim and provide a certificate on request.

7. DATA
The Producer may export its account data in full at any time through the Harbor producer portal.
