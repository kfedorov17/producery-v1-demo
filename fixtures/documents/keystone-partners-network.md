KEYSTONE INSURANCE PARTNERS - PRODUCER NETWORK AGREEMENT

1. RELATIONSHIP
Keystone Insurance Partners LLC ('Keystone') grants the Agency access to Keystone's carrier panel subject to the terms herein. All carrier appointments obtained through Keystone remain the property of Keystone.

2. BOOK OF BUSINESS
Business written through Keystone carrier appointments shall be jointly held. Upon termination, the Agency may repurchase full rights to its expirations for a fee equal to 1.5 times the trailing twelve months of commission on such business.

3. COMPENSATION
The Agency shall receive seventy percent (70%) of new business commissions. Renewal commissions shall be split with sixty-five percent (65%) to the Agency.

4. FEES
A monthly membership fee of $650 applies. An initiation fee of $7,500 is due at signing, of which $2,500 is refundable if the Agency withdraws within 90 days.

5. TERM
This Agreement has an initial term of sixty (60) months and renews automatically for successive twenty-four (24) month terms.

6. TERMINATION
The Agency may terminate only upon one hundred twenty (120) days' written notice following the initial term. Keystone may terminate at any time upon thirty (30) days' notice.

7. NON-COMPETITION
For thirty-six (36) months following termination, the Agency shall not write property and casualty business with any carrier on Keystone's panel within the Agency's operating states, and shall not solicit any account serviced through Keystone for the same period.

8. ERRORS & OMISSIONS
The Agency shall maintain E&O coverage of at least $500,000 per occurrence.

9. DATA ACCESS
Client data resides in Keystone's agency management system. Upon termination the Agency may request reports of accounts it originated; bulk export is at Keystone's discretion.
